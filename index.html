Contactless Pulse Recorder

Camera-based remote photoplethysmography (rPPG)

This is not an ECG. A camera picks up blood-volume changes in skin (a pulse wave), not the heart's electrical activity. It gives you heart rate and beat timing, but no P/QRS/T waveform. Research use only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis. The "rhythm view" below draws a generic ECG beat (averaged from other people) at each detected pulse. It shows when your heart beats, not what your ECG looks like, and it looks normal even when the real ECG is not.

Tips for a clean signal
  • Even, steady, front-facing light. Avoid flicker from LED or fluorescent lights and avoid backlight.
  • Keep still and don't talk. Sit about 40–70 cm from the camera.
  • Show your forehead (move hair aside) and take off glasses if they cause glare.
  • Wait about 10 s for the reading to settle. Record at least 60 s if you want HRV.
